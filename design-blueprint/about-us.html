Welcome to Perfecto Events

Est 2013

Our Journey

Our story began in 2013 as Regius Events, when we proudly coordinated our first wedding for a Nigerian couple in the diaspora at the prestigious Sheraton Hotel. This milestone launched our journey into destination weddings, mastering the fusion of cultural heritage and modern sophistication. By 2017, we expanded our expertise within Nigeria and rebranded as Perfecto Events, embracing a broader vision of event curation and execution. Today, we are a powerhouse of expertise, offering tailored services, including End-of-Life Ceremonies, Conferences, Hybrid Projects, Social Celebrations, Team-Building Events, Event Design, and Immersive Travel Experiences.

what defines us

Our approach is rooted in presence and meticulous attention to detail—from inquiry and conceptualization to strategy, planning, execution, and insightful post-event analysis. Every moment we craft is impactful, intentional, and tailored to your vision.

A Brief Speech From The Founder

Hello, I’m Oreoluwa, or OM, the Founder and Experience Designer behind Perfecto Events. With a background in economics and specialized training from top event academies, I blend creativity, precision, and global expertise to elevate every event. Having contributed to international projects like the Ubuntu Pathways Event and the Felix Project Fashion Show in the UK, I’ve learned that successful events are built on collaboration, humility, and bold ideas. At Perfecto Events, we bridge the gaps in the event process – ensuring flawless execution from start to finish. Our goal is not just to execute events but to craft experiences that inspire, engage, and leave a lasting impact. Event management, to us, is about curating moments that connect people, spark emotions, and create unforgettable memories. From the first conversation to the final detail, we are dedicated to transforming your vision into reality. Let’s create something extraordinary together – precision, purpose, and excellence guaranteed! Let’s bring your vision to life with precision, purpose, and excellence.
