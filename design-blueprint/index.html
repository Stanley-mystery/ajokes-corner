Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and support.

What types of events do you manage?

We manage all kinds of events including weddings, corporate functions, birthdays, conferences, and private parties.

Do you offer custom event packages?

Yes, we tailor every event package to fit your specific needs, preferences, and budget.

How far in advance should I book your services?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance, especially for larger events or during peak seasons.

Can you help us find and book event venues?

Definitely! We have strong partnerships with a wide range of venues and can assist in finding the perfect location that fits your theme, budget, and guest size.

Can you handle event decorations and vendor coordination?

Absolutely! We take care of everything from venue setup to managing vendors so you can enjoy your event stress-free.

Do you offer on-site coordination during the event?

Yes, our team is present on-site to ensure everything runs smoothly, manage last-minute changes, and provide real-time support during the event.
