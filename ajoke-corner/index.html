About Tomilade Adebari

Tomilade Adebari is an enthusiastic child of God who believes in spreading the gospel of Jesus through various means. She began work in ministry as a teenager through serving in prayer and intercession. Tomilade has a passion for praying the Word of God and seeing the world and the lives of those around her transformed through prayer. She has served in youth ministry for many years, and has a desire to plant the Word of God in the hearts of the young people of this generation. Together, she and her husband serve in marriage ministry and are devoted to helping others build marriages on Christ.

In addition to her passion for ministry, she is a board certified Comprehensive Ophthalmologist & Glaucoma specialist. She and her husband established a non-profit organization, Love Vision Foundation that provides financial support for global missions. Tomilade is happily married to Olufunbi and they are blessed with three wonderful children.

About Her

A Calling in Medicine

My passion for medicine began at the age of five, when my father—God bless his sweet memory—was diagnosed with kidney failure. What followed was a three-year journey of dialysis every other day, frequent hospital visits, and, eventually, a life-changing kidney transplant. Although I did not fully understand the medical complexities at the time, I made a promise to him, and to myself, that one day I would become one of the people in the “white coats”—caring for others and helping restore them to health. That promise continues to guide me, along with a deep commitment to ensuring that underserved communities have access to the same quality of care my father received.

I went on to earn my bachelor’s degree in Biological Sciences with a minor in Public Health, which strengthened my passion for advocacy and community-centered care. At 16, I was introduced to ophthalmology while shadowing a local ophthalmologist, and I immediately felt a connection to the field. From that moment on, every opportunity to observe—especially cataract surgeries—only deepened my fascination. There is something truly remarkable about helping someone regain their sight, and that sense of purpose has never left me.

Today, I am a board-certified ophthalmologist and glaucoma specialist. Medicine is my passion and ophthalmology is my calling. I have been blessed, by the special grace of God, with the opportunity to help others enjoy the gift of sight—allowing them to fully experience the beauty of life and the world around them.
